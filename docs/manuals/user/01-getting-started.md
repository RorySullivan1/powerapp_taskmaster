# 1 · Getting started

## Opening the app

Taskmaster runs in Power Apps. Open it the way your desk shares it — the Power Apps mobile or
web player, or a link someone sent you. You are already signed in as your work account; the app
uses that to work out which projects, tasks and issues are yours. There is no separate login and
no user profile to set up.

The layout is designed for a **tablet-shaped window** (landscape). It works in a browser at any
reasonable size, but a narrow phone-shaped window is not what it was laid out for.

## Moving around

Every screen carries the same blue **app bar** across the top: the product name on the left, the
screen you are on, and a hamburger button that opens the navigation fly-out.

The fly-out has four destinations:

| Entry | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your own work — what you lead, what you own, what is due |
| **Reports** | Desk-wide charts across all live projects |
| **Projects** | The browser for every project, with search and filters |
| **Reference** | Clients and products |

Choosing an entry navigates and closes the fly-out. Tapping outside it closes it without moving.

Screens that are *not* in the fly-out — the create/edit forms for a project, task, transaction,
issue, client or product — are reached from the screen the thing belongs to. That is deliberate:
you edit a task from its project, not from a menu.

## Conventions every screen shares

- **`*` marks a required field.** The app will not save without it.
- **Save is always clickable.** If something required is missing, pressing Save tells you what —
  it does not sit greyed-out leaving you to guess. Look for the **"Still required:"** line.
- **Cancel discards.** Nothing you have typed on a form is written until you press Save.
- **Delete asks first.** A confirmation appears over the screen; nothing is deleted until you
  confirm it there.
- **Saving is not instant.** The button reads *"Saving…"* while the write is in flight. If the
  write fails, the screen tells you and stays open with your entries intact — it does not
  navigate away and lose them.
- **Dates** are entered with a date picker, and blank is allowed wherever the field is optional.
- **People** come from the company directory. You cannot create a person in this app; you pick
  one that exists.

## Where your changes go

Straight to SharePoint, on Save. There is no draft state, no sync step and no offline queue. If
two people edit the same record, the later save wins for the fields it writes.
